Remote Work and Travel Policy Guidelines - Acme Corp

Acme Corp supports a flexible workspace and provides reimbursements for business travel and remote work setups.

1. Remote Work Stipend:
All full-time remote employees are eligible for an annual stipend of $500 to cover office equipment, ergonomic chairs, and broadband internet. Receipts must be uploaded to the expensing portal before December 1st of each calendar year.

2. Business Travel Mileage Reimbursement:
Employees using personal vehicles for business travel are reimbursed at a rate of $0.67 per mile. Daily travel logs indicating start/end coordinates and odometer screenshots must be submitted.

3. Meal Allowances:
Acme Corp provides a daily per diem allowance of $75 for meals during business travel. This is capped at $75 per day and does not require itemized receipts unless a single transaction exceeds $25. Purchases of alcoholic beverages are strictly excluded from reimbursement.

Policy Administrator: HR Benefits Division (benefits@acmecorp.com)
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
