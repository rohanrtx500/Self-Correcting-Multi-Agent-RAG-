# Global HR Policy & Employee Benefits Guide 2026

## 1. Remote Work & Equipment Allowances
- **Home Office Setup Allowance**: $1,500 one-time reimbursement upon hire for desk, ergonomic chair, and monitor setup.
- **Monthly Connectivity Stipend**: $120/month for high-speed internet and mobile expenses.
- **Coworking Space Allowance**: Up to $350/month for shared office memberships (WeWork, Regus).

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Parental Leave
- **Flexible PTO**: Unlimited annual vacation days with a mandatory minimum requirement of 15 days per year.
- **Parental Leave**: 20 weeks of fully paid leave for birth or adoptive parents, available after 90 days of employment.
- **Sabbatical Policy**: 4 weeks of paid sabbatical after 4 consecutive years of service.

## 3. Professional Development & Wellness
- **Annual Learning Budget**: $2,500 per employee per year for courses, certifications, and conferences.
- **Mental Health Days**: 4 company-wide wellness days per calendar year.
- **HR Operations Lead**: Brenda Chen (`hr-benefits@company.com`).
